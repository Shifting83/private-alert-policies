# Private Alert Service SMS Terms

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

These terms govern SMS alerts sent by the Private Alert Service, a private, owner-operated location-based incident alert program.

## Program description

The service sends low-volume operational notifications when a public-source incident report matches locations configured by the owner. A message may contain a short incident summary and a link to related details or media. The program does not send marketing messages.

## Consent and eligibility

The sole intended subscriber is the service owner. The owner opts in by entering their own mobile number in the private application settings and enabling SMS delivery. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

## Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with matching incident activity and the owner's settings. Message and data rates may apply according to the subscriber's wireless plan.

## Opt-out and help

Reply **STOP** to cancel messages. After a STOP request, one final confirmation message may be sent. Reply **HELP** for help. The owner may also disable SMS delivery, remove the number, or stop the application.
