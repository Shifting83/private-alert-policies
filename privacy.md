# Private Alert Service Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Private Alert Service handles information for its private, owner-operated location-based SMS alert program.

## Information collected

The service may process:

- The mobile phone number entered by the owner
- Alert locations and notification preferences configured by the owner
- Public-source incident information used to generate alerts
- Basic delivery, error, and diagnostic information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service

## How information is used

Information is used only to configure, generate, send, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot requested operational alerts.

## Information sharing

The service uses Twilio and participating wireless carriers to deliver text messages. Information may be provided to those service providers only as needed to transmit and support the messages.

Mobile information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its owner, or others.

## Data retention and security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve problems, and enforce applicable terms. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Your choices

The owner may stop SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP**, disabling SMS delivery, removing the destination number, or shutting down the service. Reply **HELP** for assistance.
