# Private Alert Service Owner-Only SMS Opt-In

This is a private, self-hosted alert program with no public subscriber form or third-party recipient list. The sole intended recipient is the owner and operator of the service.

## How the owner opts in

The owner gives express consent by:

1. Opening the private application's SMS notification settings
2. Entering their own mobile phone number
3. Enabling SMS delivery
4. Saving the settings

By completing these steps, the owner requests recurring operational location-based incident alerts at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

## Message details

Message frequency varies according to matching public-source incident activity and configured locations. Messages may contain links to incident details or media. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time. Reply **HELP** for help. The owner can also stop messages by disabling SMS delivery, removing the phone number, or shutting down the application.

Mobile information and opt-in consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
